## Team Leader responsibilities

- Schedule and coordinate the weekly meeting.
- Prepare the agenda.
- Record minutes, decisions, and actions.
- Confirm task owners and due dates.
- Review blockers and overdue work.
- Update backlog and project records.
- Place High and Critical risks on the agenda.
- Confirm the next meeting's priorities.
- Check contribution evidence.

## Handover

At the end of each leadership period, the outgoing leader will provide:

- Open actions
- Current blockers
- Risk updates
- Schedule changes
- Backlog status
- Overdue tasks
- Decisions awaiting approval
- Evidence requiring completion
