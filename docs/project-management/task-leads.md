## Lead responsibilities

The lead will:

1. Confirm scope.
2. Break the deliverable into tasks.
3. Confirm dependencies and supporting members.
4. Track progress and blockers.
5. Maintain completion evidence.
6. Present the deliverable for team review.
7. Record approved changes.

## Changing a lead

The team must record any lead change in:

- Meeting minutes
- Product backlog
- GitHub issue or project item
- Responsibility matrix
- Task-lead table
- Microsoft Project schedule
- Risk register, when affected
- Contribution log
